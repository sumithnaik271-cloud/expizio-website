Frequently asked questions

Can I start with just one service?

Yes. Many clients start with one thing, like ads or a website, and add more once they see it working.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what you need. After a short call we send a simple proposal with the scope, timeline and budget, so you know the cost before anything starts.

How soon will I see results?

Ads can start bringing enquiries soon after they go live. SEO and organic social build up more slowly. We tell you what to expect for your business before we begin.

Which areas do you serve?

We focus on Udupi, Manipal, Mangaluru and the towns across coastal Karnataka. If you're further away, message us and we'll tell you honestly whether we're a good fit.

How will I know it's working?

Every month you get a plain-language report: enquiries, cost per enquiry, and what we will change next.
